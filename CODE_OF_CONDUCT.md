# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are dedicated to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for all contributors, maintainers, and users of StanClip regardless of background, identity, or technical experience.

## Community Standards

Positive behaviors include:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others
- Giving and graciously accepting constructive feedback
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and skill levels
- Focusing on what is best for the overall user community and project

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- Trolling, disruptive behavior, or bad-faith discussions
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement and Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening a private communication with the project maintainers. Maintainers will review and address any issues fairly and promptly.
